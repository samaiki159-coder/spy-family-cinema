SPY × FAMILY

A FAMILY BUILT ON SECRETS.

A spy. An assassin. A telepath. An unexpected family.

01
THE MISSION

IT ALL STARTED WITH A MISSION.

Twilight is one of the most skilled spies in Westalis. His mission is simple—but almost impossible.

To protect the peace between two nations, he must get close to an important political figure. However, there is only one way to do it.

He must create a family.

Using the identity of Loid Forger, he adopts a young girl named Anya and later enters a marriage with Yor.

But there is one problem.

Loid does not know that Anya can read minds.

And Anya does not know that Yor is secretly a deadly assassin.

Together, they begin Operation Strix.

A mission created to protect the world.

But somewhere along the way, their fake family begins to feel real.

02
THE FAMILY

FOUR PEOPLE. FOUR SECRETS. ONE FAMILY.

LOID FORGER

Code Name: TWILIGHT

A brilliant spy with countless identities.

Loid Forger is calm, intelligent, and always prepared for every situation. Behind his perfect appearance is Twilight, one of the most skilled agents of WISE. His mission requires him to become a husband and father. But as time passes, the mission becomes more complicated. Because being a spy may be easier than being a parent.

YOR FORGER

Code Name: THORN PRINCESS

A gentle woman with a dangerous secret.

Yor works an ordinary job during the day, but behind her peaceful appearance is one of the most skilled assassins in Ostania. She agrees to become Loid's wife for her own reasons. What begins as a fake marriage slowly gives her something she never expected. A place where she belongs.

ANYA FORGER

Secret: THE TELEPATH

A little girl who can hear everyone's thoughts.

Anya knows the truth about her parents. She knows that her father is a spy. She knows that her mother is an assassin. And because she loves exciting adventures, she thinks this is the most amazing family in the world. Her biggest secret mission? Keep the Forger family together.

BOND FORGER

Secret: THE FUTURE

A loyal dog with the ability to see the future.

Bond becomes the newest member of the Forger family after meeting Anya. Quiet, gentle, and intelligent, Bond may not speak—but he sees things that others cannot. Together with Anya, he sometimes helps protect the family from danger.

THE THEMES

MORE THAN JUST A MISSION.

I

FAMILY

Family is not always about blood. Sometimes, it is about the people who unexpectedly enter your life and choose to stay.

II

TRUST

The Forgers keep secrets from one another. Yet despite everything they hide, they slowly learn how to depend on each other.

III

PEACE

Behind Loid's mission is a simple dream. A world where children do not have to experience the pain of war.

IV

IDENTITY

Everyone in the family lives behind a mask. But as they spend more time together, they slowly discover who they truly want to become.

V

LOVE

The Forger family was never supposed to be real. But somewhere between missions, school, danger, laughter, and everyday life—they begin to care for one another.

"A mission brought them together.
But family made them stay."

GALLERY

A COLLECTION OF MEMORIES.

THE MISSION CONTINUES.

Their family was supposed to be fake.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

This website is a fan-made creative project inspired by the anime and manga Spy × Family.

It explores the story of the Forger family through themes of family, identity, trust, peace, secrets, and unexpected connections.
